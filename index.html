House rules

Spanish 40-card deck with 8 reyes and 8 ases: threes count as kings and twos count as aces. Partners sit opposite each other. Play goes counter-clockwise: the mano sits on the dealer's right and speaks first in the mus round and in all four lances.

Mus: everyone says Mus or No hay mus in turn. If all four say Mus, each player discards 1–4 cards; discards go to a pile, and when the deck runs out that pile is shuffled into a new deck. The first No hay mus starts the betting.

Lances: Grande (high cards), Chica (low cards), Pares, Juego (31 or more: 31, 32, 40, 37…33). If nobody has juego, Punto is played (closest to 30). Ties go to whoever is closest to the mano. Before Pares and Juego, each player says sí or no in turn.

Envido opens at 2; you can raise, accept (Quiero) or refuse (No quiero). A refused bet scores the previous stake (1 for a first bet). Accepted bets and passed lances are counted at the end of the hand. Bonuses: par 1, medias 2, duples 3; juego 2, 31 scores 3; punto 1. An accepted Órdago wins the game on that lance. Each game is to 40; a match is the best of the chosen number of games.

Coins: each team's points are shown as coins. One partner holds the blue coins (1 point each), the other holds the red coins (5 points each).
